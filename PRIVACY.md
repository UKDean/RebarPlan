# Privacy and support

## Privacy

RebarPlan runs entirely on your computer.

- It sends no data anywhere. There is no tracking, no analytics, no ads and no account.
- Your projects are saved only where you choose to save them (`.rebarplan` files), plus an automatic copy in the app's own local storage on your computer so an unsaved schedule survives a restart.
- "Send by email" hands the exported Excel file to the mail program on your computer. RebarPlan itself does not send email and never sees your address book.
- Links in the app (this page, the website, GitHub) open in your browser only when you click them.
